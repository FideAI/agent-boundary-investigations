## Included and excluded

Included: Fide's analytical code, synthetic fixtures and local experiment journals; derived factual tables, source identifiers and hashes; short field labels and source locators needed to inspect the claims; original Fide interpretations and limitations.

Excluded: full URLQuery JSON reports, Transluce's original dataset ZIP/catalog, historical response bodies, full third-party articles, archived executable payloads, private workspace paths, credentials and internal program coordination. The acquisition helper obtains pinned public report JSON separately and never executes submitted programs.
